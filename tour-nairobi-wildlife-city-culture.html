Family Safari · Culture

Nairobi Wildlife, City & Culture Experience

5 Days / 4 Nights  |  Argyle Grand, Nairobi

About This Package

A paced, family-friendly introduction to Nairobi’s wildlife, history, and living culture. Over five days you combine a game drive in the world’s only national park inside a capital city with hand-feeding Rothschild giraffes, meeting orphaned elephants, colonial-era heritage at the Karen Blixen Museum, and traditional dance at Bomas of Kenya — all from a comfortable base at Argyle Grand.

Highlights

  • Game drive in Nairobi National Park with the city skyline as your backdrop
  • Hand-feed endangered Rothschild giraffes at the Giraffe Centre
  • Watch orphaned baby elephants at the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust
  • Dinner at Carnivore Restaurant — Nairobi’s iconic open-fire nyama choma experience
  • Immersive culture at Bomas of Kenya with traditional homesteads and live dance

Accommodation

Four nights at Argyle Grand, Nairobi — a comfortable city base for rest between wildlife and culture days.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1 — Arrival in Nairobi

    Morning: Arrival at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport. Meet-and-greet by your representative and transfer to Argyle Grand for check-in and rest.

    Afternoon: A relaxed, light introductory activity in Nairobi to ease into the trip after travel — options include a stroll through a local craft market or a short orientation drive through the city, paced for children.

    Evening: Dinner at the hotel.

    Overnight: Argyle Grand, Nairobi

  2. Day 2 — Nairobi National Park & Carnivore

    Morning: After breakfast, depart for a game drive in Nairobi National Park — the world’s only national park within a capital city. Look out for lions, rhinos, giraffes, buffalo, and abundant birdlife, all with the city skyline as a backdrop.

    Afternoon: Lunch en route or at a local restaurant, followed by free time back at the hotel to relax.

    Evening: Dinner at the Carnivore Restaurant — Nairobi’s iconic open-fire nyama choma experience, a memorable treat for the whole family.

    Overnight: Argyle Grand, Nairobi

  3. Day 3 — Karen Blixen Museum & Giraffe Centre

    Morning: Visit the Karen Blixen Museum, the former farmhouse of the author of Out of Africa, for a walk through Kenya’s colonial-era history and beautifully preserved gardens.

    Afternoon: Lunch at a nearby restaurant, then continue to the Giraffe Centre, where the family can hand-feed and get up close with endangered Rothschild giraffes — a firm favourite with children.

    Evening: Dinner at the hotel.

    Overnight: Argyle Grand, Nairobi

  4. Day 4 — David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust & Bomas of Kenya

    Morning: Visit the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust to watch orphaned baby elephants being fed and playing in the mud — a heart-warming, gentle-paced activity ideal for younger children.

    Afternoon: Lunch at a local restaurant, then head to Bomas of Kenya for an immersive cultural experience: traditional homesteads from Kenya’s various communities and a live tribal dance and music performance.

    Evening: Dinner at the hotel.

    Overnight: Argyle Grand, Nairobi

  5. Day 5 — Departure

    Morning: Breakfast at the hotel, check-out, and transfer to the airport for departure.
