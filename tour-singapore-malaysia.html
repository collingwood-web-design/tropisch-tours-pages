Singapore & Malaysia Explorer

7 Days / 6 Nights  |  Singapore & Kuala Lumpur

Singapore

Malaysia

About This Package

Discover the best of Southeast Asia on this seamlessly curated Singapore & Malaysia Explorer Package. Combine Singapore’s iconic skyline, world-class attractions, and vibrant island life with Malaysia’s cultural landmarks, hilltop adventures, and thrilling theme parks. From Sentosa Island to the Batu Caves and Genting Highlands, this trip is designed for travellers who want two dynamic destinations in one unforgettable getaway.

What to Expect

  • Singapore highlights — Night Safari, Universal Studios, panoramic city drive, Sentosa Island experiences, and Mandai Bird Paradise
  • Malaysia adventures — Kuala Lumpur city tour, Batu Caves, Genting Highlands cable car, and a full day at Sunway Lagoon
  • Seamless logistics — return economy flights, hotels with daily breakfast, and private airport transfers at both destinations

Accommodation

3 nights in Singapore and 3 nights in Kuala Lumpur, with daily breakfast included.

  • Days 1–3: Singapore (3 nights)
  • Days 4–6: Kuala Lumpur (3 nights)
  • Day 7: Departure

Itinerary

Singapore (3 nights)  ·  Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (3 nights)

  1. Day 1 — Arrival in Singapore & Night Safari

    Arrive at Singapore Changi Airport. Meet and greet by our staff, then transfer to your hotel for check-in. In the evening, enjoy a Night Safari with tram ride.

  2. Day 2 — Universal Studios Singapore

    Spend a full day at Universal Studios Singapore with entrance included.

  3. Day 3 — City Drive & Sentosa Island

    Enjoy a 3-hour Singapore panoramic city drive, followed by a Sentosa Island tour including cable car, 4-in-1 Madame Tussauds + Images of Singapore + Boat Ride + Ultimate Film Experience, and the Wings of Time Show.

  4. Day 4 — Mandai Bird Paradise & Fly to Kuala Lumpur

    Check out and visit Mandai Bird Paradise, then transfer to Singapore Changi Airport for your flight to Kuala Lumpur. On arrival at Kuala Lumpur International Airport, meet and greet, transfer to your hotel for check-in, and enjoy a half-day Kuala Lumpur city tour.

  5. Day 5 — Genting Highlands & Batu Caves

    Full-day Genting Highlands tour, en route via the Batu Caves, including a 2-way cable car ride with entrance tickets.

  6. Day 6 — Sunway Lagoon Theme Park

    Full-day Sunway Lagoon Theme Park visit with entrance tickets included.

  7. Day 7 — Departure

    Check out and transfer to Kuala Lumpur Airport for your return flight home.
