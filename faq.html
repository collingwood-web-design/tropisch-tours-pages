Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about booking Kenya safaris, visas, and travel with Tropisch Tours & Travel.

Safari and travel questions

Who is Tropisch Tours & Travel?

Tropisch Tours & Travel is a Nairobi-based safari and travel agency specialising in Kenya wildlife safaris, beach holidays, and tailor-made East Africa itineraries. We are headquartered at Hazina Towers, 19th Floor, Nairobi, Kenya.

Do you offer tailor-made Kenya safaris?

Yes. We design bespoke itineraries around your dates, budget, and interests — from Maasai Mara and Amboseli safaris to Mombasa and Diani beach escapes and fully custom routes across East Africa.

How do I book a safari with Tropisch Tours & Travel?

Call +254 703 112 833, email tropischtours@gmail.com, or use our contact form. A travel specialist will confirm availability, pricing, and next steps.

What languages do your guides speak?

Our expert guides speak English as standard. Other languages can be arranged on request for private groups and tailor-made journeys.

Can you combine safari and beach holidays?

Yes. Many guests pair a wildlife safari with time on the coast — for example Maasai Mara or Amboseli followed by Mombasa or Diani. Our Amboseli · Tsavo West · Taita Hills package also offers an optional North Coast beach extension.

Do I need a visa or eTA to visit Kenya?

Most international visitors need a Kenya Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) before travel. Many African passport holders are visa-exempt for stays up to 90 days. Apply via the official portal at etakenya.go.ke at least two weeks before departure, with a passport valid for six months or more. Carry your approved eTA (printed or on your phone) for immigration at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport.

Is visa or eTA cost included in your packages?

No. Visa fees, eTA charges, and airport taxes are excluded from all Tropisch Tours & Travel package prices. Each guest arranges and pays for their own entry authorisation.

When is the best time to visit Kenya for a safari?

Safaris run year-round. Dry seasons (June–October and January–February) offer excellent wildlife viewing. The Maasai Mara Great Migration peaks roughly July–October. Green seasons (March–May, November) mean lush scenery, fewer crowds, and superb birdlife.

What currency should I bring?

Kenya uses the Kenyan Shilling (KES). USD and major credit cards are accepted at hotels and larger venues. Package prices are quoted in USD. We recommend some local currency for tips, crafts, and incidentals.

Do I need travel insurance?

Yes — comprehensive travel insurance is strongly recommended and is not included in our packages. Ensure cover for medical emergencies, evacuation, cancellation, and personal belongings.

Do I need a yellow fever vaccination?

You may need a yellow fever certificate if arriving from or transiting through an endemic country. Rules vary by nationality and route — check etakenya.go.ke and consult your doctor before travel.

What health precautions should I take?

See your doctor or travel clinic 4–6 weeks before travel about malaria prophylaxis — risk varies by region (higher on the coast and some bush areas than in Nairobi). Drink sealed bottled or filtered water; avoid tap water unless your hotel confirms it is safe. Our packages include bottled water on game drives.

Which airport should I fly into?

Most international flights arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA), Nairobi (NBO). Some domestic and fly-in safaris use Wilson Airport. Share your flight number and arrival time when booking so we can arrange meet-and-greet or transfers. We recommend arriving on Day 1 in the morning or early afternoon, and allowing at least three hours before international departures.

Are safaris suitable for children?

Many packages welcome families. Selected itineraries offer child rates (typically ages 2–11) and free travel for infants under 2. Some activities suit certain ages better than others — tell us your children’s ages when enquiring. Child car seats for private transfers can be requested in advance, subject to availability.

How do deposits and payments work?

Your booking is confirmed once you receive written confirmation and any required deposit has been received. Deposit amount, balance due date, and accepted methods (such as bank transfer or card) are confirmed at booking. Prices are quoted in USD. See our Terms & Conditions for full payment and cancellation details.

What should I wear and pack for a safari?

Wear neutral-coloured, layered clothing, a wide-brim hat, sunscreen, sunglasses, and comfortable closed shoes. Bring a light jacket for early-morning drives. A soft bag or medium suitcase suits road safaris. Fly-in packages (such as our Masai Mara Flying Safari) often limit luggage to around 15 kg per person in soft bags — we will confirm limits for your itinerary.

How much should I tip guides and drivers?

Gratuities are not included in package prices. Tipping is discretionary but appreciated. A common guideline is USD 10–15 per guest per day for a safari guide and USD 5–10 per guest per day for a driver, in USD or Kenyan Shillings — adjust based on service and group size. Lodge staff may be tipped separately at your discretion.
