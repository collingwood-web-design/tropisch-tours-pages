Zanzibar Beach Packages

About These Packages

Trade Nairobi for the spice island with two mid-range Zanzibar beach options from Tropisch Tours & Travel. Both packages include return international flights (Nairobi ↔ Zanzibar), airport transfers, accommodation, and selected excursions — so you can unwind on white-sand beaches, explore Stone Town, and taste island culture with logistics handled from the start.

Package 1 — 4-Day Zanzibar Escape

4 Days / 3 Nights  ·  Blue Turaco Zanzibar  ·  Best for short beach holidays and couples

  1. Day 1 — Arrival in Zanzibar

    Arrive in Zanzibar. Airport transfer to Blue Turaco Zanzibar. Check in and enjoy the rest of the day at leisure on the beach.

  2. Day 2 — Beach Leisure

    Full day at leisure enjoying the beach, swimming, and hotel facilities at your own pace.

  3. Day 3 — Stone Town & Spice Farm

    Explore historic Stone Town and visit a spice farm. Return to the beach hotel for the evening.

  4. Day 4 — Departure

    Check out and transfer to the airport for your flight back to Nairobi.

Package 2 — 5-Day Zanzibar Beach & Culture

5 Days / 4 Nights  ·  Baraza Resort / Zuri Zanzibar or similar

  1. Day 1 — Arrival & Beach Transfer

    Arrive in Zanzibar and transfer to your beach resort. Check in and settle into island time.

  2. Day 2 — Beach Leisure

    A full day to enjoy the beach, pool, and resort facilities at leisure.

  3. Day 3 — Stone Town & Spice Farm Tour

    Discover Stone Town’s history and culture, then visit a spice farm before returning to the coast.

  4. Day 4 — Mnemba Island Snorkeling & Dolphins

    Snorkel at Mnemba Island and enjoy a dolphin experience on the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean.

  5. Day 5 — Departure

    Check out and transfer to the airport for your return flight to Nairobi.
