Dubai Family Beachfront Escape

5 Days / 4 Nights  |  Centara Mirage Beach Resort  |  Travel 1 Nov – 5 Dec 2026

About This Package

Centara Mirage Beach Resort offers a beachfront island setting with unlimited water park access, making it an excellent family-friendly escape. This 4-night Dubai package pairs a 5-star family resort stay on a bed & breakfast basis with private tours of the city, the desert, and Dubai Marina — so the whole family can play, relax, and explore with every detail taken care of.

What to Expect

  • Beachfront island resort — a 5-star family resort at Centara Mirage Beach Resort, Dubai
  • Unlimited water park access — included throughout your stay
  • Private tours — Dubai City Tour, Desert Safari with BBQ Dinner, and Marina Dhow Cruise with Dinner, all on a private basis
