Nairobi National Park Half-Day Game Drive

About 5 Hours  |  Morning or Afternoon  |  Daily Departures

About This Package

Watch lions, rhinos and giraffes roam the savannah with the Nairobi skyline rising behind them. Nairobi National Park is the world’s only national park bordering a capital city, just minutes from the city centre, and it is one of the easiest ways to have a real safari in Kenya. Your private vehicle and driver-guide collect you from your accommodation and bring you back, park fees are included, and you choose a morning or afternoon departure.

Kenya’s first national park, opened in 1946, is a protected stretch of open plains, acacia woodland and forest. It is a strong place to see black rhino, and its hippo pools and birdlife make every drive different. It suits first-time safari-goers, families, business travellers and anyone with a layover or only half a day to spare.

What to Expect

  • Private game drive — your own expert driver-guide in a private safari vehicle
  • Wildlife with a skyline — lion, black rhino, giraffe, buffalo, zebra and, with luck, cheetah, framed by the Nairobi city skyline
  • Hippo pools & birdlife — hippo pools and over 400 bird species for birdwatchers

Itinerary

Choose a morning or afternoon departure. Daily at 6:00 AM or 2:00 PM.

Morning departure
  1. 6:00 AM — Pickup

    Pickup from your accommodation in Nairobi.

  2. 6:45 AM — Enter Nairobi National Park

    Arrive at the park gate and begin your safari.

  3. 6:45 – 10:45 AM — Private game drive

    Explore the plains, woodland and hippo pools with your driver-guide.

  4. 11:30 AM — Drop-off

    Return to your accommodation in Nairobi.

Afternoon departure
  1. 2:00 PM — Pickup

    Pickup from your accommodation in Nairobi.

  2. 2:45 PM — Enter Nairobi National Park

    Arrive at the park gate and begin your safari.

  3. 2:45 – 6:15 PM — Private game drive

    Afternoon wildlife viewing across the park.

  4. 7:00 PM — Drop-off

    Return to your accommodation in Nairobi.
